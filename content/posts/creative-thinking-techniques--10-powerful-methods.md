Your calendar is full, the problem is unclear, and the obvious ideas have already surfaced. Whether you are working alone or with a group, this is the point where **creative thinking techniques** become useful, not theoretical.


Creative work is rarely a matter of waiting for inspiration. Strong thinkers, like strong teams, rely on repeatable methods to generate options, challenge assumptions, and move from vague ideas to practical decisions. That is important because creativity is not fixed. A major review of adult creativity interventions found that **70.09%** of participants in creativity-enhancement conditions performed better than the average person in control groups ([repository summary of the meta-analysis](https://media.walling.io/journal/2026/08/creativityenhancementmethods_accepted-2.pdf?ref=walling.io)). In practical terms, creative performance can improve when the process improves.


A clear visual workspace makes that process easier to sustain. In Walling, ideas can be captured as bricks, organized across multiple views, and kept visible in one shared environment instead of being scattered across notes, chat threads, and disconnected documents. That is where structured creativity becomes a practical workflow for individuals and teams.

## 1. Brainstorming


![0a1119e6-dbad-4fd6-b056-d3e3deb71d94.jpg](/images/posts/01deb235b67dc254.jpg)


Brainstorming remains one of the most useful creative thinking techniques because it is simple, flexible, and easy to apply. For an individual, it creates momentum when a blank page or difficult decision feels stuck. For a team, it helps surface a wider range of ideas before the group starts evaluating them.


The quality of the session depends on structure. Start with one focused question, set a clear time limit, and separate idea generation from judgment. During the first phase, aim for volume. During the second, review, group, and assess the strongest options.


**How to use it well**

- Define one specific problem or opportunity
- Set a timer, often 10 to 20 minutes
- Capture every idea without filtering
- Group similar ideas after the session
- Evaluate only after the idea list is complete
